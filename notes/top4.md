# Top 4 (after revision). A = pick.

A
---
Controls

You have control, he said.
I have control, I said.
You have control, he said,
the way they teach it, three times,
so the plane is never no one's.

He lifted his hands from the yoke
and set them on his knees.
Forty years he had flown.
He looked out the window.
---

B
---
Crosscut

The long saw has one rule:
you only pull.
My first stroke, I pushed.
The blade bowed and stuck.
Across the log he waited.
Only pull, he said.
All right.

Then the teeth went
mine, his, mine,
and the oak came apart
with neither of us pushing.
---

C
---
Take

Halfway up, forearms gone,
I looked down at her.
Take, I said.
Got you.
The rope went tight.

The hard part was opening my hands
on rock I had fought for.

I sat back into the air.
She walked me down,
her weight doing my climbing.
---

D
---
Sheet

Off the line, still cold,
it hung between us,
two corners each.
We pulled. It cracked flat.
Fold? she said. Fold.
I walked my corners into her hands.

She held all four.
I held nothing,
and with nothing
I opened the drawer.
